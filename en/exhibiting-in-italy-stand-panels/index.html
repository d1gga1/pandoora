Why source stand walls locally in Europe

A US booth rebuilt for a European show usually hits three walls at once: freight (a container each way), dimensions (European stands are planned in metres, with different height rules), and fire documentation — Italian and European venues ask for materials classified under EN 13501-1 or the Italian Class 1 system, not US test reports. Buying the walls in Italy removes all three.

We deliver directly to the pavilion, to your European stand builder or to a warehouse, with our own vehicles, not only by courier — and our fire-rated panels come with the documentation: Class 1 and Euroclass B-s1,d0, the MDF supplier's certificate and our declaration of conformity on every delivery.

European stand rules in two minutes

  • Metric sizes. Stock panels are 2,500–5,000 mm long and 250–1,000 mm wide, 40 mm thick; custom sizes up to 1,200 mm wide cost the same.
  • Fire. Organisers ask for the reaction-to-fire class of walls and ceilings, often at project approval. Decide the fire-rated finish at order stage. See our fire rating guide.
  • Timing. Standard sizes 2–3 working days, custom 7 days, RAL lacquer 10–12 days — then delivery inside the build-up slot the organiser gives you.

Frequently asked questions

We are a US company exhibiting in Milan. Can you deliver to our stand builder?

Yes — to the pavilion within your build-up slot, to your European stand builder or to a warehouse, with our own vehicles.

Will our US fire certificates be accepted in Italy?

Usually not as such: Italian and European venues ask for a reaction-to-fire classification under EN 13501-1 or the Italian Class 1 system. Our fire-rated panels are documented as Class 1 and Euroclass B-s1,d0.

Do you quote in dollars?

We quote in euros, in English, with each item priced separately and the delivery terms stated.

Can you store the panels between European shows?

Ask us when you order: tell us the next show and we will tell you what we can arrange for that project.
