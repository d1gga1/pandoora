The venue and what it asks for

Spring Fair and Autumn Fair are the shows where our slatwall panel earns its place: on a gift or homeware stand the wall is not a backdrop, it is the display, and the layout gets rearranged during the show itself. Being able to move a shelf by hand rather than drill for it is worth more than it sounds on a five-day run.

For the larger builds at Autosport and the Caravan show, the two positions that need deciding from the drawing are the levelling platforms for the raised floor and the reinforced panels wherever a screen or a bracket is hung. Both cost very little in the factory and a great deal on site.

The main shows

The shows we are most often asked to supply at this venue:

  • Spring Fair and Autumn Fair — home, gift and retail: hundreds of compact stands, heavy display duty on the walls.
  • Autosport International — motorsport: large plots, heavy exhibits, raised floors.
  • Glee — garden and outdoor living, with large product displays.
  • The Caravan, Camping & Motorhome Show — big footprints and long open runs of wall.

What we supply for a stand

What leaves Fontanelle for a build is almost always the same package:

  • standard panel PSL for the walls, 40 or 50 mm, up to 6,000 mm high;
  • the fire-rated finish wherever the venue regulations ask for it, with the documentation;
  • stand doors, assembled and with the hardware fitted, reversible, with porthole or panic bar;
  • levelling platforms, pre-drilled for the adjustable feet, for the raised floor;
  • alignment strips, I·L·T·X connectors and cover strips for dry assembly.

How we deliver and in what time

We manufacture in Fontanelle, in the province of Treviso, and the trucks leave from there. Birmingham is 1,630 km away: a run we make regularly, with direct delivery into the hall within your build slot, or to your warehouse if you would rather pre-build the stand.

Deliveries to Birmingham sit outside the EU customs union, so the movement carries an export declaration on our side and an import entry on yours. That is a matter of a few days of lead time, not an obstacle — but it has to be planned before the truck is loaded, not while it is at the border. Tell us the consignee and the customs broker at order stage and we prepare the transport documents around them.

Standard sizes are always in stock. We normally ship within 7 days of order confirmation, and in practice usually sooner. No minimum order, no surcharge for non-standard sizes.

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver directly to the halls in Birmingham?

Yes, we deliver into the hall within your build slot, or to your warehouse if you would rather pre-build the stand.

How long does delivery take?

Standard sizes are always in stock. We normally ship within 7 days of order confirmation, usually sooner. For deliveries abroad we agree the slot and the transport documents in advance.

Are the panels available fire-rated?

Yes, with the fire-performance documentation supplied at no extra cost. Doors, platforms and lacquered panels are available fire-rated too.

Do you work to the stand builder's drawing?

Yes, we make to measure, including single pieces, with no surcharge for non-standard sizes: you pay the price of the next standard size up.

Pricing a stand in Birmingham?
