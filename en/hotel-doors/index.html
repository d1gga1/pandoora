Doors for hotels, guesthouses and apartments

A guest-room door works harder than any door at home: opened dozens of times a day, it has to keep corridor noise out and still look right season after season. We make them in our factory in Fontanelle, Italy, for hotels, guesthouses and serviced apartments across Europe.

What a hotel door really needs

Quiet rooms

Between room and corridor, the first thing guests notice. Solid-core leaf, seals on the rebates and a drop seal or threshold to close the gap under the door.

Hard-wearing faces

Laminate shrugs off suitcases and housekeeping trolleys and wipes clean. For public areas, RAL lacquer that can be touched up years later.

Fire safety

Where the fire strategy calls for it, EI fire-rated doors, also in timber, matched to the other doors on the floor (see fire doors).

One consistent look

Room, bathroom and back-of-house doors in the same finish and the same production cycle, even when ordered at different times.

How we work with hotels

  • Door schedule: for every room, opening size, wall thickness, handing and type: hinged, sliding for small bathrooms, flush-to-wall for suites.
  • Finish sample to approve before production on multi-room orders.
  • Labelled by room: every door leaves the factory marked with its room number.
  • Delivery in lots, floor by floor, so the rest of the property can stay open.
  • Installation by your local fitter, with our measuring form and remote support; in north-east Italy by our own team.
  • Spares: the same finish can be reproduced years later to replace a single leaf.

Frequently asked questions

Can you supply a whole hotel?

Yes, from a few guesthouse rooms to a multi-storey hotel. Send the door schedule with sizes and handing and we quote line by line, usually within 24 working hours.

How much do hotel room doors cost?

From €395 per door (laminate) to €695 (flush-to-wall), excluding VAT, with frame, architraves, hinges, magnetic lock and handle. Transport outside Italy is quoted separately.

Can the doors cut corridor noise?

Yes: solid-core leaf, perimeter seals and a closed gap under the door. The result also depends on the wall, so we check it against your drawings.

Do you make fire-rated hotel doors?

Yes, EI doors where the fire strategy requires them, also in timber and matched to the room doors.

Have a hotel project?

Send the door schedule or the drawings: we answer with a written quote, usually within 24 working hours, in English or Italian.
