Company

Working wood to measure since 1987

Pan.door.a S.r.l. is a Treviso-based manufacturer specialising in hollow-core panels, doors, flooring and furniture components. We combine the craft of the joinery with the consistency of industrial production.

About us

We were founded in 1987 in Fontanelle, in the province of Treviso, at the heart of one of the densest furniture districts in Europe. Since then we have done one thing, and we have done it consistently: turning wood into finished elements that have to fit together perfectly — a six-metre stand wall, a lacquered door that has to align to the millimetre, a door that has to close in silence.

We work for two different worlds with the same need for precision: stand builders, who have immovable installation dates, and furniture companies, who entrust us with semi-finished parts and finishes. In both cases the yardstick is the same: tight tolerances and deadlines met.

Quality and materials

We work to FederlegnoArredo quality standards and use Class 1 adhesives. The panel frame is finger-joint fir, a choice that guarantees dimensional stability over time even on extended lengths; the honeycomb core keeps the weight low without giving way in flatness.

For trade-fair setups we make the fireproof versions required by exhibition-venue regulations and the reinforced versions for walls that have to carry loads, screens or equipment.

Where we work

We serve the whole of Italy and ship abroad. We work regularly with exhibitors and stand builders at the main Italian trade-fair venues — Fiera Milano, VeronaFiere, BolognaFiere and IEG Expo — and we speak Italian, English, French and German.
