Floors and walls, indoors and outdoors

The same tiles go on the floor, on the walls and inside the shower: the easiest way to get a bathroom in one continuous finish. Porcelain stoneware does not stain, does not absorb water and resists limescale, frost and heavy foot traffic, so it also suits kitchens, terraces, shops, restaurants and hotels.

Custom washbasins in the same finish as the floor

We cut the same slabs you choose for the floor and join them with 45° mitred edges, so the veining wraps around the corners without a break. We make:

  • Wall-hung and countertop washbasins
  • Vanity tops and clad bathroom cabinets
  • Kitchen worktops and islands
  • Shower trays
  • Tables
  • Fireplace and counter cladding

Worldwide shipping and a showroom in Milan

We ship tiles and washbasins, carefully packed, anywhere in the world. Our showroom is in San Giuliano Milanese, just outside Milan, open at weekends too by appointment. Choose from photos, on a video call or from samples, and we will send you a quote.

Frequently asked questions

Can the same tiles be used on floors and walls?

Yes. Every collection can be laid on floors and walls, showers included. For terraces and pool surrounds choose the R10 anti-slip outdoor series.

Can the washbasin match the floor exactly?

Yes. It is made from the same porcelain slabs as your floor and walls, so colour and veining are identical.

How long does delivery take?

Material is ordered to your choice; if you are in a hurry we can have everything ready in about a week, plus shipping time abroad.

Do you ship outside Italy?

Yes, we ship tiles and custom washbasins worldwide. Installation is available in Milan and Lombardy, Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia.
