Made-to-measure wooden furniture for hotels and homes

Shoe cabinets, bathroom vanities, bedside tables, headboards, desks, wardrobes and wall units in lacquered MDF, melamine, HPL laminate or lightweight honeycomb board. Made to measure or in series, also in the same colour as the doors, and delivered flat-packed with instructions.

The furniture every home and hotel room needs

Besides doors we make the wooden furniture a home or a hotel room needs. It can be made in the same finish as the doors, so the room is coordinated without looking for another supplier. We make both the single made-to-measure piece and series of dozens of identical pieces, and there is no minimum order.

Hotel and B&B room furniture, in series for every room or made to measure.

Which material to choose

RAL-lacquered MDF

Seamless surface in any RAL colour, matt or gloss, lacquered in our paint shop. The choice for matching furniture to lacquered doors.

Melamine-faced board

Practical and economical, plain or wood-effect. Suited to series production and interiors.

HPL laminate

Resists scratches, knocks and humidity. Designed for hotels, short lets and bathrooms, where furniture works hard.

Lightweight honeycomb board

Light but rigid panels for large doors, headboards and walls, easier to ship and assemble.

Flat-packed, with instructions

We ship furniture flat-packed, with assembly instructions, as the big furniture brands do. The reason is practical: an assembled cabinet takes up far more space in transport than a flat pack, and on a series of dozens of pieces the difference in shipping cost is significant. Flat packs are easy to store on site and are assembled where they are needed.

If you prefer not to handle it, our team can assemble on request, at an extra cost shown in the quote.

Furniture for hotels, B&Bs and short lets

For hospitality we make room furniture in series: bedside tables, headboards, desks, bathroom vanities and wardrobes identical for every room, coordinated with the hotel doors. You can start from one of our base models and customise size, colour and handles, or from the interior designer's drawing.

Documentation on the reaction to fire of the materials is available on request and is issued after the order, once the goods have been delivered.

Custom or in series, no minimum order

Some pieces have standard models that can be repeated in series or customised. For everything else we work to measure or to drawing. There is no minimum order: a single piece or a whole hotel. For volume orders we apply the trade price list.

Frequently asked questions

What wooden furniture do you make?

Shoe cabinets and hallway units, bathroom vanities, bedside tables, headboards and desks for hotels, wardrobes, walk-in closets, wall units, shelves, tables and accessories.

Is the furniture delivered assembled?

No, it arrives flat-packed with instructions. It takes far less space in transport, so shipping costs less. Assembly is available on request at extra cost.

Which materials do you use?

MDF lacquered in any RAL colour, melamine-faced board, HPL laminate and lightweight honeycomb board. For hotels and bathrooms we recommend HPL or lacquer, for economical series melamine.

Is there a minimum order?

No, you can order a single piece. For volume orders we apply the trade price list.

Do you have standard models or only custom?

Both: base models that can be repeated in series and customised, or furniture made to measure or to drawing.

Do you provide reaction-to-fire documentation for hotels?

Yes, on request. Documentation on the materials is issued after the order, once the goods have been delivered.

Can the furniture match the doors?

Yes. Doors and lacquered MDF furniture go through the same paint shop and match in the same RAL colour.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, we ship flat-pack furniture across Italy, Europe and to non-EU countries. The flat-pack format makes shipping simpler and cheaper.
