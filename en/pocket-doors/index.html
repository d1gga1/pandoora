How a pocket door works

A steel pocket frame is built into the wall before it is boarded or plastered. The leaf slides inside it and leaves the opening completely clear: no swing, no lost floor space in front of the door.

It is the natural choice for small rooms, en-suites, walk-in wardrobes and corridors where a hinged door would get in the way.

Before you order

  • The pocket frame goes in before the wall is closed: plan it with the build or the refurbishment.
  • We need the structural opening sizes and the finished wall thickness.
  • Next to the opening the wall needs a clear length at least equal to the door width.
  • A pocket door is less soundproof than a hinged door with seals: for bedrooms and offices see our acoustic doors.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a made-to-measure pocket door cost?

€245 ex VAT per door in laminate and €445 in RAL lacquer, leaf and pocket frame included: a pocket door costs the same as a hinged one. Glass adds €150. Delivery is priced in the free quotation.

Can a pocket door go into an existing wall?

The wall has to be opened to fit the pocket frame. If you want to avoid building work, a surface-mounted sliding door on a visible track is the alternative.

Are pocket doors soundproof?

Less than a hinged door with perimeter seals. For bedrooms and offices an acoustic hinged door works better.

Do you make double pocket doors?

Yes, with two leaves opening in opposite directions.

Do you install in the UK and Ireland?

No: we supply and ship, without installation. Your fitter gets a survey sheet and fitting instructions with every door.

Doors for a project in the UK or Ireland?

Send the door schedule or the drawings: we reply with a written quotation, normally within 24 working hours, in English or Italian.
