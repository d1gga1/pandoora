The venue and why it changes the build

Dubai Exhibition Centre (DEC) sits inside Expo City Dubai, in Dubai South, close to Al Maktoum International Airport and to the Jebel Ali port area. The shows moving there are among the biggest in the region — which means large custom stands, tall walls carrying heavy screens and build-up windows that do not move.

The real novelty is that several shows now split across two venues: WHX and Gulfood run at both Dubai World Trade Centre and DEC. A stand builder working both sites in the same week benefits most from a modular system — the same standard panels, the same connectors, the same doors — rather than two sets of one-off carpentry.

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply stand builders at Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo City?

Yes. The panels, doors and platforms are the same we supply for Dubai World Trade Centre and European shows, shipped to your Dubai workshop or to the venue through your forwarder.

Can I use the same panels at DWTC and Expo City in the same week?

Yes. It is a modular system: walls are dismantled, moved and rebuilt with the same connectors.

Can the walls carry large LED screens for GITEX?

We supply reinforced panels with internal reinforcement where the screen fixings go. Send us the load and the fixing points with the drawing.

Are the panels fire-rated?

The fire-rated finish is available on walls, doors and platforms, with the documentation included.
