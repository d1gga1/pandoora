What a flush door is

A flush, or hidden, door has its leaf in the same plane as the wall: no architrave, no visible frame, no step between door and wall. It works thanks to a concealed aluminium frame built into the wall during construction or refurbishment, which disappears once the wall is skimmed and painted.

The leaf hangs on adjustable hidden hinges and opens normally, push or pull, with or without a handle.

What it needs

  • A plan: the frame goes in when the wall is built or rebuilt.
  • Walls that are straight and plumb: on a flush door any defect shows.
  • Careful skimming around the frame, then paint on the wall and, if you like, on the leaf.
  • The opening direction (push or pull) decided when you order.

Finishes

Leaf sprayed in any RAL or NCS colour, or supplied primed and ready to paint with the wall. A flush door can also slide: see our pocket doors.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a flush internal door cost?

€445 ex VAT primed and €645 sprayed in RAL, per door, concealed frame, hidden hinges and magnetic lock included. Delivery is priced in the quotation.

Can a hidden door be fitted in a finished house?

Yes, but the wall has to be opened to build in the frame, then skimmed and repainted. Decide before you decorate.

Can a flush door be painted like the wall?

Yes: we supply it primed and ready to paint, or sprayed in the RAL or NCS colour of the wall.

Is a flush door flush on both sides?

It is flush on the side where the leaf sits in the wall plane; the other side shows the door like a normal one. You choose push or pull when you order.
