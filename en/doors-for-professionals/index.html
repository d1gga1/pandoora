Interior doors for trade professionals

We have been making made-to-measure doors since 1987 and supply them directly to people who work in housing and hospitality. You get our 33 catalogue models plus anything you need beyond the catalogue. From 20 doors there is a trade price list.

A manufacturer, not a middleman

Pan.door.a designs, builds and lacquers its doors at its factory in Fontanelle, near Treviso, Italy. For trade customers that means one contact for everything: non-standard sizes, RAL colours, construction variants and deliveries. And because we make the doors, we can tell you straight away whether a solution is feasible, without going through a dealer.

The starting point is the 33 models in our 2026 catalogue, in three lines: laminate, lacquered raised-panel in any RAL colour and CNC-engraved, available as reversible hinged, sliding or flush-to-wall doors. Beyond the catalogue we produce on request:

  • models to the architect's or designer's drawing, from a dimensioned elevation or a reference;
  • replicas of existing doors, for renovations where new doors must match the originals;
  • technical doors: fire-rated (EI/REI), acoustic, security doors and floor-to-ceiling doors.

Prices for volume: the trade price list

Our public prices are designed for private customers: one home, a few doors. When the numbers grow, so does the logic of production: a batch is cut, lacquered and packed in series, and the price reflects that.

That is why, from 20 doors per project, we work with a trade price list reserved for professionals. We do not publish it: we send it on request to VAT-registered companies, together with a quote for the project.

What we offer professionals

Samples and sample kits

Finish and colour samples to approve before production, and a physical sample kit for those who need to show it to clients.

Data sheets, DWG and BIM

Dimensioned sheets, sections and files to drop the doors into your drawings and specifications.

Phased, labelled deliveries

On long projects we deliver floor by floor or by batch. Every door is labelled with its unit and opening.

One project contact

A single person follows quote, samples, production and deliveries: no bouncing between departments.

Italy, Europe and beyond

We ship across Italy, Europe and outside the EU. Abroad we supply with a survey sheet for your local installer.

Standard sizes in stock

Many standard-size doors are in stock and ship straight away. Custom sizes, special finishes and technical doors have lead times agreed and written into the quote.

Not just doors: wooden furniture for homes and hotels

Besides doors we make wooden furniture: shoe cabinets and hallway units, bathroom vanities, bedside tables, headboards and desks for hotels, wardrobes, walk-in closets and wall units. They can be made in the same colour as the doors and are delivered flat-packed with instructions, which keeps shipping costs down. Everything is on the page wooden furniture for hotels and homes.

How a supply starts

  1. You send us the project. Door schedule, specification, floor plan with numbered openings or even just a list: any format works.
  2. Quote on trade prices. Separate items for leaf, frame, architraves, hardware, finish and installation, with technical notes on any variants.
  3. Samples. Where needed, a finish sample or sample door to approve before production.
  4. Production and deliveries. Within the lead times agreed in the quote, in batches if needed, with every door labelled by opening.

Frequently asked questions

Why are trade prices different from public prices?

Public prices are designed for private customers buying a few doors. From 20 doors per project production is organised in batches and we apply a trade price list, sent on request to VAT-registered companies.

From how many doors does the trade price list apply?

From 20 doors per project. Below that, our public prices apply.

Can you make models that are not in the catalogue?

Yes. Besides the 33 catalogue models we make doors to drawing, replicas of existing doors and technical doors: fire-rated EI/REI, acoustic, security and floor-to-ceiling doors.

Do you work with real estate agencies and interior designers?

Yes: real estate agencies, architects, interior designers, general contractors, building companies, showrooms and hotels. We pay a commission to anyone who refers a client to us.

Can you supply doors without your brand?

Yes. Interior designers, showrooms and dealers can receive doors without the Pan.door.a brand and offer them to their clients under their own name.

Do you also make furniture?

Yes: shoe cabinets, hallway units, bathroom vanities, bedside tables, headboards and desks for hotels, wardrobes, walk-in closets and wall units, delivered flat-packed with instructions.

What are the lead times for a supply?

Many standard-size doors are in stock for immediate delivery. For custom sizes, special finishes, technical doors and large quantities, lead times are set for each project and written into the quote; on long projects deliveries are scheduled floor by floor or by batch.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes, across Italy, Europe and to non-EU countries. Outside north-east Italy we supply with a survey sheet for the installer you choose.

Do you provide samples and technical files?

Yes: finish samples, sample kits, data sheets, DWG and BIM files. Each project has a single contact person.
