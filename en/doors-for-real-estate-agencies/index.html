For real estate agencies

Interior doors for real estate agencies

A home with old doors immediately looks like it needs work. We work with agencies to refresh properties before they go on sale, to supply new developments and to furnish them quickly. And we pay a commission on every client referred to us.

Doors change how a home is perceived

Buyers notice doors straight away: they touch them, open them and see them in almost every listing photo. Yellowed, chipped or dated doors make a property look like a renovation project, and that weighs on the price. Replacing them is one of the fastest ways to make a flat look new before it goes on the market.

We are the manufacturer: the same models we sell to private customers we supply to agencies, with dedicated terms when the numbers justify it.

Four ways to work with an agency

Upgrades before sale or letting

We replace the doors in the properties on your books, choosing neutral, current models that appeal to more buyers.

Supplies for new developments

For buildings you are marketing: a standard specification model plus the options buyers can choose, at agreed prices.

Referrals with commission

If a client buys a home and wants new doors, refer them to us: we pay a commission on referred clients, on terms agreed at the start of the partnership.

Home staging and short lets

Flat-pack wooden furniture such as shoe cabinets, hallway units, bedside tables and bathroom vanities, to make a property presentable or ready to let.

Which doors we recommend for properties on sale

Buyers want a door that does not force them to redo anything else: white or a neutral colour, clean lines and hardware that feels solid. For flats meant for letting, including short lets, we recommend the laminate line, which stands up to scratches and heavy use. For premium properties there is the RAL lacquered or CNC-engraved line, also flush-to-wall.

The starting point is the 33 models in our 2026 catalogue, in three lines: laminate, lacquered raised-panel in any RAL colour and CNC-engraved, available as reversible hinged, sliding or flush-to-wall doors. Beyond the catalogue we produce on request:

  • models to the architect's or designer's drawing, from a dimensioned elevation or a reference;
  • replicas of existing doors, for renovations where new doors must match the originals;
  • technical doors: fire-rated (EI/REI), acoustic, security doors and floor-to-ceiling doors.

See our made-to-measure interior doors.

Prices for agencies

Our public prices are designed for private customers: one home, a few doors. When the numbers grow, so does the logic of production: a batch is cut, lacquered and packed in series, and the price reflects that.

That is why, from 20 doors per project, we work with a trade price list reserved for professionals. We do not publish it: we send it on request to VAT-registered companies, together with a quote for the project.

Furniture for home staging, delivered flat-packed

An empty property sells worse than one furnished with a few well-chosen pieces. We make shoe cabinets, hallway units, bathroom vanities, bedside tables, headboards, wardrobes and wall units in wood, also in the same colour as the doors. They arrive flat-packed with instructions: they take little space in transport and are assembled on site. Assembly by our team is available on request, at extra cost. All our wooden furniture.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with real estate agencies?

Yes: we upgrade doors in properties for sale or rent, supply the new developments you market, make flat-pack furniture for home staging and pay a commission on referred clients.

How does the referral commission work?

If you refer a client who needs to replace or buy doors, we pay you a commission. Terms are agreed at the start of the partnership.

Which doors should go into a home for sale?

Neutral, current models, usually white or a light colour, with clean lines. For rental properties we recommend the laminate line, which is more resistant to heavy use.

Why are trade prices different from public prices?

Public prices are designed for private customers buying a few doors. From 20 doors per project production is organised in batches and we apply a trade price list, sent on request to VAT-registered companies.

Do you supply furniture for home staging?

Yes: shoe cabinets, hallway units, bathroom vanities, bedside tables, headboards and wardrobes in wood, delivered flat-packed with instructions. Assembly is available on request at extra cost.

Can you supply a whole new development?

Yes, with a standard specification model and the options buyers can choose. Deliveries are scheduled floor by floor or by batch, with every door labelled by unit and opening.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes, across Italy, Europe and to non-EU countries. Outside north-east Italy we supply with a survey sheet for the installer you choose.
