MDF cut to size

We cut, edge, drill, rout and spray MDF to the sizes you give us, from a single panel to a production batch. Panels arrive square and finished, ready to fit: for furniture makers, joiners, shopfitters, stand builders and, on request, homeowners.

What we do to it

Cutting to size

Cut and squared to the finished size, with production tolerances even on a one-off.

Edging

Edge banding on visible sides, or square edges lacquered with the face.

Drilling

Holes for hinges, shelf pins, connectors and cable outlets, to spec.

CNC routing

Curves, shapes, joints and grooves from DWG or DXF files, repeatable across a batch.

Panel routing

Shaker frames and grooves routed into the face for doors and panelling.

Spray lacquering

Primer, sanding and top coat in our spray shop: one face or both, edges included.

How to order

  • Send a cutting list with finished sizes, thickness, quantity, board type and finish; for shapes a DWG or DXF file.
  • You get feasibility, lead time and price, usually the same day.
  • Lacquered parts typically take 10–12 working days; raw cut parts less. Exact times are in the quote.
  • Shipped from Italy, packed for transport, across Europe and to the UK and Ireland.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cut MDF to size for a single panel?

Yes, one-offs are welcome: it is the usual case for anyone with a non-standard size.

Which thicknesses do you have?

We work the main commercial thicknesses; tell us what you need and we confirm availability and lead time in the quote.

Can you lacquer both faces?

Yes, one face or both, edges included, in any RAL or NCS colour, matt, satin or gloss.

Do you have fire rated MDF?

Yes, Euroclass B-s1,d0 to EN 13501-1, with supplier certificate and declaration of conformity on every delivery.

Do you ship to the UK?

Yes. UK deliveries are imports, so duty and import VAT may apply; see our UK shipping and customs page.
