Why weight and order matter more in New York

The Javits Center hosts shows such as the New York International Auto Show, the Summer Fancy Food Show and design and gift shows. Freight reaches the hall through scheduled marshalling and dock slots, material handling is billed by weight, and I&D labor is billed by the hour. Every one of those costs goes down when the walls are light and the crew does not have to work out which panel goes where.

So for New York we do two things by default: we number every panel to your drawing and pack the crates in installation order, and we suggest folding columns wherever a column is needed — they travel flat and take a fraction of the space.

Frequently asked questions

Can you pack the crates in installation order?

Yes. Every panel is numbered to your drawing and the crates are packed and labelled so the crew unpacks in the order it builds.

How light are honeycomb panels compared with MDF walls?

A honeycomb panel weighs a fraction of a solid MDF or plywood wall of the same size, because the core is a paper honeycomb inside a spruce frame. Material handling at US shows is billed by weight, so the saving shows up on the drayage invoice.

Can you deliver to the Javits Center?

Yes — through your forwarder or ours, to the advance warehouse, your exhibit house or the show site, following the marshalling instructions in the exhibitor manual.
