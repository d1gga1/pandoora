Contract manufacturing

What we supply, and what we don't

We manufacture the visible parts of furniture on a contract basis: doors, drawer fronts, worktops, side panels, shelves, wall panelling and boiserie — finished, squared, drilled to your drawing and ready to fit. We do not build the carcass: that stays with the joinery or furniture maker who commissions us, which is precisely why we are never in competition with our own customers.

Lacquered doors

MDF or hollow-core substrate depending on thickness and format, full lacquering cycle, square or radiused edges to the design.

Drawer fronts

Matched to the doors in the same shade and the same sheen — on a kitchen, the risk to avoid is fronts and doors coming from two different finishing runs.

Routed doors

Grooves and frame profiles machined on CNC and then lacquered: the classic or continuous pattern without an applied frame.

Worktops and sides

Worktops, exposed sides, top rails and plinths, dimensioned and finished together with the doors so the whole unit reads as one.

Shelves

Made in hollow-core construction: the thickness follows the span and the load, and concealed fixings are built in at order stage, not improvised on site.

Large formats

Doors and panels beyond standard sizes, on the same presses and in the same finishing department as our trade-fair panels.

Ordering

What a good schedule contains

The fastest way to a quotation is a component schedule: finished sizes, thickness, RAL shade, sheen, edge type and drilling for each piece. A cutting file or a drawing works just as well.

Two things we always ask, because they are what causes problems. First, the grain or pattern direction on veneered or routed pieces. Second, which faces are visible: the finish of backs and hidden edges is a cost decision and should be made deliberately, not by omission. If your reference shade has no RAL code, send the physical sample — we identify the closest match and tell you honestly how close it is, before the whole batch goes into production.

Frequently asked questions

Do you make the furniture as well?

No — only the visible components: doors, fronts, worktops, sides, shelves and boiserie, finished and ready to fit. The carcass is built by the joinery or furniture maker who commissions us.

What colours can you lacquer?

Any shade in the RAL range, gloss, satin or matt, plus UV gloss for surfaces that need to be very hard and brilliant. If your reference is a physical sample rather than a code, send it: we identify the closest shade and say honestly how close it is.

What do you need for a quotation?

A schedule with finished sizes, thickness, RAL shade, sheen, edge type and drilling per piece — plus the grain direction on veneered or routed pieces and which faces are visible.

Do you handle large formats?

Yes: doors and panels beyond standard sizes, on the same presses and in the same finishing department we use for trade-fair panels. It is one of the main reasons customers come to us.

Can components match the doors you make?

Yes, and they pass through the same finishing cycle — the only reliable way to be sure that doors and furniture fronts are genuinely the same shade and the same sheen.

Furniture components

Made-to-measure semi-finished parts for furniture makers

For furniture makers, joineries, interior designers and design studios we produce the elements that make up the finished piece: over 200 items in 24 categories, all available made to measure from the client's drawing.

Those who come to us usually have the same problem: an order with non-standard elements that is not worth producing in house. Those are what we work on: single pieces or small batches, with the same tolerances as industrial production.

Doors and fronts

Lacquered doors in RAL shades, drawer fronts, glazed frames, routed doors. Edge-banding and drilling carried out to specification.

Panels and surfaces

Worktops, tops, side and back panels, made-to-measure shelves and wall panelling, in hollow-core or solid panel according to the expected load.

Structures

Bed frames, headboards, bases and load-bearing structures made to drawing, ready to be assembled on site or in the workshop.

Decorative accessories

Mouldings, cover strips, shaped elements and finishing details, matched to the lacquering of the main components.

How contract work works

The process is straightforward: you send us the drawing or the measurements, we check feasibility and reply with a quotation — normally within 24 working hours. We accept DWG, PDF and even dimensioned hand sketches.
