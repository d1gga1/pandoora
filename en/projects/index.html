How a setup comes together

Behind every one of these projects is the same chain: hollow-core panels for the walls, levelling platforms and joint components for the structure, hollow-core doors for the entrances, and in-house lacquering to bring everything into line with the brand's identity.

Working with a single supplier from cutting to packing is what makes it possible to meet build dates that cannot move. If you have a trade fair in the diary, let's talk.
