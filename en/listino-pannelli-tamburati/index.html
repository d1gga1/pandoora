What the range covers

This page is the technical reference for the complete stand range: panels (raw, laminated, fire-rated, Electric, Arte, reinforced, grid and 6-metre), doors (raw, laminated, fire-rated, with porthole, panic, sliding and double), levelling platforms and the accessories that hold a build together: vertical alignment cores, I, L, T and X connectors, trim edges and end-cap trims.

Every item is listed with its description, technical drawing and price list where available. Non-standard sizes and one-off pieces are produced to the customer's drawing, and we deliver throughout Italy and the European Union — including direct delivery to the exhibition venue.

How the panel is specified

The data sheet matters more than the price when comparing suppliers: a panel that arrives out of square is paid for in assembly hours, not in euros of supply.

Standard specification of the hollow-core panel
ItemSpecificationWhy it matters
FrameKnot-free finger-jointed spruce, full-length stavesCarries the fixings, keeps the panel square and lets the edge be machined without opening the core.
CorePerforated high-grammage honeycombFills the panel without weight: it is why a 6-metre element is still a two-person handle.
FacingsSanded before bondingAn uneven facing shows through any finish, especially a gloss lacquer.
End ventsOn every panelLet gases escape during pressing and cooling. Without them, panels blister.
AdhesivesClass 1 vinylQuality and safety, and the reason a panel survives repeated builds.
Thicknesses40 and 50 mm50 mm for long spans or hung loads; 40 mm for standard partitions.

Ordering

Placing an order

To quote we need quantities and sizes per item, thickness, finish (raw, laminated or lacquered with the RAL code), whether internal reinforcements are required and where, and the delivery date. A component schedule or a cutting list is faster than any description. Written quotation within 24 working hours.

On reaction to fire, venue technical regulations vary from one event to another: check the regulation for your show, tell us the class required, and the certification travels with the goods so it is available at project approval.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order non-standard sizes?

Yes. Panels, doors and platforms are produced to non-standard sizes and as one-off pieces, to the customer's drawing or specification. Send the component schedule with the sizes and we will confirm feasibility and lead time in the quotation.

Are the technical drawings available?

Yes — dimensioned drawings are downloadable from the relevant product tabs on this page, and the remaining ones are supplied on request together with the assembly sheet.

Do the doors come with hardware?

Doors are supplied pre-assembled with frame and leaf and all necessary hardware, handle excluded. Locks are available in PATTERN or YALE versions according to requirements.

What sizes are the levelling platforms?

Three standard sizes — 498 × 498, 998 × 498 and 998 × 998 mm — plus made-to-measure. They are supplied squared and drilled for adjustable feet, which are available on request.

Do you deliver directly to the exhibition venue?

Yes, throughout Italy and worldwide, including direct delivery inside the venue within your build-up window. Tell us the delivery date when you request the quotation: it determines whether the production slot is realistic.
