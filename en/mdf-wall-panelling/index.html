MDF wall panelling

Wall panelling in lacquered MDF, built to the real size of the wall: classic panels with mouldings, fluted or slatted walls, or large plain panels. Sprayed by the same people who lacquer our doors, so panelling and doors can share one colour.

Styles

Classic panelling

Raised or applied mouldings, skirting and dado rail: the panelling of period homes and traditional hotels.

Fluted and slatted

Vertical slats or flutes at a regular pitch, the most requested finish for TV walls, headboards and hallways.

Plain panels

Large panels with fine shadow gaps and no mouldings: a clean finish that hides services and uneven walls.

Pilasters and capitals

Decorative MDF elements for classic interiors and high-end hospitality projects.

How we design it

Panelling that works is planned on the wall, not in a catalogue. Before we make anything we need the real wall size and the position of sockets, switches, radiators and windows: the joints and frames are drawn around them.

  • Height: full height, half height (usually 100–120 cm) or up to the cornice.
  • Skirting: built into the panelling, or matching the doors.
  • Fixing: on battens or with concealed systems, so panels can come off if a service needs reaching.
  • Finish: spray lacquer in any RAL, matt, satin or gloss; or primed, ready to paint on site.

We make it in MDF because it is stable, takes lacquer well and routes cleanly; very large panels are built with a honeycomb core, lighter on the wall.

Who we make it for

Joiners, interior designers, architects and fit-out contractors send us the drawing and fit on site: we supply numbered panels, lacquered and ready to install. We also make panelling for homeowners on request. Shipped from Italy across Europe and to the UK and Ireland.

Frequently asked questions

What is the panelling made of?

Lacquered MDF, or honeycomb core for very large panels. Veneered or primed versions on request.

Can it match the colour of the doors?

Yes, we lacquer it in the same shop and in the same RAL colour as our doors.

How are sockets and switches handled?

They are surveyed before design; frames and slats are laid out around them and cut-outs are made in production.

Do you make panelling for hotels and shops?

Yes, also in series for every room or store, with numbered panels for installation.

Do you install it?

Outside north-east Italy we supply panels ready for your joiner or fitter to install.
