Fire-rated doors and Dubai Civil Defence

In Dubai the reference is the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code of Practice, enforced by Dubai Civil Defence. Two points save a lot of time on a hotel project. First, what is tested and certified is the door set — leaf, frame, hinges, seals, closer and any glazing together — not the leaf on its own: changing the hardware after the fact can take a door outside its certification. Second, UAE consultants typically work with test standards such as BS EN 1634-1 or UL 10C, and ask for certification and labelling on leaf and frame.

Our fire-rated doors are EI certified and supplied with their certification and test documentation. Send us the specification, the fire strategy and the hardware schedule at tender stage: we confirm in writing which rating and which configuration we can certify — before the order, not while the consultant is reviewing the submission.

Approvals are project-specific and go through the project consultant and Dubai Civil Defence.

Quiet rooms sell better

Noise from the corridor and from the next room is one of the most common complaints in guest reviews. A hollow door lets sound through; a door that insulates needs three things together — mass in the leaf (a solid, high-density core), seals on the rebates and a drop seal closing the gap under the leaf. The wall and the installation matter just as much, which is why we look at the door together with the wall build-up rather than promising a figure from a catalogue.

Frequently asked questions

Are your hotel doors fire-rated?

Yes, our fire-rated doors are EI certified and supplied with their certification and test documents. We confirm the rating and configuration against your specification at tender stage.

Are your doors approved by Dubai Civil Defence?

Approval is given per project through the consultant and Dubai Civil Defence. We provide the certificates and test reports needed for the submission and tell you in advance whether the specified configuration is covered.

Can the doors be prepared for electronic locks and closers?

Yes. Leaves and frames are machined to the hardware schedule: send us the models of lock, closer, hinges and viewer.

Do you make acoustic connecting doors?

Yes, with a solid high-density core, perimeter seals and a drop seal. The result also depends on the wall and on installation, which we review with you.

Can you supply a mock-up room?

Yes. On hotel projects we produce sample doors in the final finish and hardware for approval before series production.

Do you also supply serviced apartments and branded residences?

Yes — the same door families, often with more lacquered and flush-to-wall doors in the units.

Pricing the doors for a hotel in Dubai?

Send the door schedule, the specification and the hardware schedule: we reply with a written quote and technical notes, normally within 24 working hours.
