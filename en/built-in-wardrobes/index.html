Built-in wardrobes made to measure

Wall-to-wall wardrobes, walk-in closets, alcoves and the space under the stairs: we build them to the real size of the room, with fronts in the same finish as the interior doors we make. One Italian workshop for doors and furniture.

What we make

Built-in wardrobes

Wall to wall and floor to ceiling, with no dead space above or at the sides. Hinged or sliding doors.

Walk-in closets

A room or a niche turned into a dressing room, with the interior laid out to your list.

Awkward spaces

Sloping ceilings, alcoves and under-stairs: exactly where a standard wardrobe does not fit.

Matching furniture

Shelving, wall panelling, headboards and hallway units in the same finishes.

Wardrobe fronts and interior doors come out of the same department and the same finishing cycle, so the white of the wardrobe is the same white as the doors.

How it works abroad

  1. Send rough measurements, photos of the wall and what the wardrobe has to hold.
  2. We reply with a design and a written quote, item by item.
  3. Final measurements are taken with our measuring form, by you or your fitter.
  4. We build in Fontanelle, Italy, and ship; your local fitter assembles with our drawings and support.

Frequently asked questions

Can the wardrobe fronts match my doors?

Yes, that is the main reason to order both from us: same laminate, RAL lacquer or CNC pattern, from the same production cycle.

How much does a built-in wardrobe cost?

It depends on size, doors and interior fittings. Send rough measurements and a few photos and we send a written quote with every item listed.

Who installs it?

In north-east Italy our own team; abroad your local fitter, with our assembly drawings and remote support.

Can you make only new fronts for an existing wardrobe?

Yes, if the carcass is sound: we make the fronts to size in the finish you choose.

Planning a wardrobe?

Send measurements and photos: we reply with a written quote, usually within 24 working hours.
