Why fire performance decides the build

A contractor building a stand answers to more than the client: they answer to the venue's technical regulations and, in the end, to the officer who signs the hall off. For materials that means demonstrated fire performance — and it has to be shown, not asserted.

In practice you get asked twice: at stand plan approval, and again at the inspection before opening. Both times what is needed is a document that travels with the material. A "fire-rated" panel with nothing to show for it is, procedurally, an ordinary panel.

That is why our fire-rated finish leaves the plant with the fire-performance documentation for the material supplied — at no extra cost and without you having to ask for it separately.

What changes against the standard panel

The construction is identical to the standard panel PSL: knot-free finger-jointed spruce frame, high-grammage perforated core, edge venting, sanded skins. What changes is the fire-retardant MDF skins; the Class 1 white glue is standard across our whole production anyway.

Sizes, widths and heights match the standard range, up to 6,000 mm. Fire-rated white and black laminate is also available on every size at extra cost — worth having when the panel has to arrive finished and stay visible, with no painting on site.

Fire-rated versions are also available for stand doors, levelling platforms and RAL-lacquered panels: on a stand with fire requirements it pays to look at the whole package, not just the walls.

What documentation we supply

With the delivery you receive the fire-performance documentation for the material supplied, in the form the stand submission needs. If your venue or your client asks for a particular format or for additional evidence, tell us when you order and not after the truck arrives: chasing a document once the stand is already standing is the worst position to be in.

Availability, special sizes and delivery

Standard sizes are always in stock. We normally ship within 7 days of order confirmation and in practice almost always sooner: in exhibition work the enquiry arrives from one day to the next, and we are set up for that.

  • Minimum order: none. We make single pieces.

Frequently asked questions
