Direct from the factory

Buying from the manufacturer, not from a reseller

We have been pressing hollow-core panels in our own plant at Fontanelle, in the province of Treviso, since 1987. Cutting, frame assembly, pressing, edging and lacquering all happen under one roof. Nothing is subcontracted, which is why an answer about a non-standard size or an unusual finish reaches you the same day, from the people who would actually make it.

Our customers are stand builders, exhibition contractors, agencies, joineries and furniture manufacturers across Italy and the European Union. We do not build stands ourselves — we supply the timber part — so we are never in competition with the companies that buy from us.

Construction

How a Pan.door.a hollow-core panel is built

A hollow-core panel is a sandwich: a perimeter frame in finger-jointed solid timber, a lightweight honeycomb core, and two facings bonded under press. That structure is what makes the panel light, dimensionally stable and — the point most people miss — survivable at dismantling, which is what decides whether a stand can be reused.

Perimeter frame

Finger-jointed solid timber. It carries the fixings, keeps the panel square and lets the edges be worked without exposing the core.

Honeycomb core

A paper honeycomb structure that fills the panel without weight. It is what allows a 6-metre element to still be handled by two people.

Thicknesses

40 and 50 mm. The 50 mm is used where the span is long or where loads will be hung from the surface.

Lengths to 6 m

Continuous walls with no visible joints. On a branded back wall this is the difference between a backdrop and a row of panels.

Internal reinforcement

Blocks positioned inside the panel exactly where screens, shelves or heavy graphics will be fixed. It must be specified at order stage.

Curved panels

Radii produced in the factory rather than forced on site. Curves are what separate a designed stand from an assembled one.

Ordering

What we need, and how delivery works

To quote we need five things: quantities and sizes for each panel type, thickness (40 or 50 mm), finish — raw, laminated, or lacquered with the RAL code — whether reinforcements are needed and where, and the delivery date. If you work from a cutting list or a component schedule, send that: it is faster than any description. Written quotation within 24 working hours.

We deliver throughout Italy and the European Union, either to the exhibition venue within your build-up window or to a holding warehouse if you prefer to pre-assemble. The critical date is rarely the delivery — it is the order: in peak season production weeks fill up, and a lacquering cycle cannot be compressed. If the design is approved, send it even months ahead; reserving a production slot costs nothing.

On reaction to fire: venue technical regulations require certified materials and the conditions vary from one event to another. Check the regulation for your show and tell us which class is required — we produce the fire-rated versions and the documentation ships with the goods.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell directly, or through a distributor?

Directly. We are the manufacturer: panels leave our plant at Fontanelle (TV) and reach you without a commercial intermediary. When you ask whether a size or an operation is possible, the answer comes from the people who would carry it out.

What thicknesses and lengths do you produce?

Forty and fifty millimetres, in lengths up to 6 metres, plus reinforced, curved and fire-rated versions. The 6-metre length is what allows continuous walls with no visible joints on a branded backdrop.

Can screens and shelves be fixed to the panels?

Yes, but the internal reinforcement has to be specified at order stage and positioned exactly where the fixings will go. Adding it afterwards means working on a finished panel, and the result shows. Send us the drawing with the load points.

Are the panels fire-rated?

We produce versions with certified reaction to fire. The class required depends on the technical regulation of the individual event: check it, tell us which class applies, and the documentation will travel with the material so it is available at project approval.

Can the panels be reused for several shows?

Yes, if they are dismantled sensibly — the perimeter frame is what lets a panel survive dismantling. On a recurring stand it pays to design an abacus of standard reusable elements plus a small amount of disposable customisation. Across three editions the difference is substantial.

Do you deliver outside Italy?

Yes, throughout the European Union, either to the venue within your build-up window or to a holding warehouse. Tell us the delivery date when you request the quotation, not afterwards: it determines whether the production slot is realistic.

Do you also build the stand?

No, and we say so up front. We manufacture the timber part — panels, walls, platforms and doors. The stand itself is built by the contractor, who is our typical customer. That is precisely why we are never in competition with the companies that buy from us.
