A door that arrives finished

The door is where most of the time gets lost on a build, because it is the one element that has to work and not merely stand: it has to close, not catch, and not swing open on its own. Adjusting a door at the show means having a joiner kneeling in front of the stand while the first visitors arrive.

Our stand doors are supplied assembled, with frame and leaf, complete with all the hardware — only the handle is missing — trimmed and grooved. On site the door goes into the wall and is aligned with the connector system.

Construction matches the panels: sanded skins, knot-free finger-jointed spruce frame, high-grammage perforated honeycomb core, edge venting, Class 1 white glue. On request we fit a PATTERN or YALE lock.

The seven versions

Reversible, raw

The standard answer: the ideal base for finishing on site. Handing is decided on the build, not in the factory.

Reversible, laminated

White, black, light grey, mid grey, dark grey and oak. Ready to use, with no finishing on site.

Reversible, fire-rated

With fire-retardant skins and fire-performance documentation. The version venue regulations ask for.

With porthole

A round glazed element: a lighter and safer doorway, because you can see who is coming the other way. Typical for the entrance to the stand store.

With panic bar

Complete with panic hardware, for escape and emergency route requirements.

Sliding

Saves floor area inside: no swing needed, which makes it the right answer for stores and services rooms.

Double-leaf

For wide entrances and set-piece arrivals, or for moving goods through during the build.

Frequently asked questions

Is the handle included?

No: the door comes complete with all the hardware except the handle, which stays with the customer. On request we fit a PATTERN or YALE lock.

What does a reversible door mean?

That the handing is decided on the build rather than in the factory: the same door can open left or right. In exhibition work that matters, because layouts change up to the last minute.

Do you make doors with panic bars?

Yes, complete with panic hardware, for escape and emergency route requirements. Available fire-rated too.

Can you make non-standard sizes?

Yes, non-standard sizes and single pieces, plus fully bespoke solutions to the customer's drawing or specification.
