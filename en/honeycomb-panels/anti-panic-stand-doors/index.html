What is an anti-panic stand door

A honeycomb door — timber frame, honeycomb core and MDF skins — fitted with a panic bar compliant with EN 1125: it opens with a push on the bar, even with full hands. It is used for emergency exits of double-deck stands, storage rooms, meeting rooms and enclosed areas of the stand.

Made to measure for your stand

We build the door to the opening: width, height and wall thickness. It ships complete with leaf, frame, hinges and panic bar, labelled per wall and loaded on the same truck as the panels. Price on quotation: send sizes, number of leaves and finish.

Frequently asked questions

Is the panic bar EN 1125 compliant?

Yes, we fit panic bars compliant with EN 1125.

Is there a fire-rated version?

Yes, the leaf can be made in fire-retardant MDF B-s1,d0, like our exhibition panels.

How much does an anti-panic stand door cost?

It depends on size, number of leaves and finish: we quote it in writing, usually within 24 working hours.
