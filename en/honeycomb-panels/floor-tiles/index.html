What floor tiles are for

The floor tile is the finished surface that goes on top of the structure: the walking surface of a raised floor sitting on the levelling platforms, the wall cladding when a different material feel is wanted from a painted panel, the quickly laid display surface on counters and plinths.

The advantage is speed: they are modules, they are laid dry and they can be swapped one at a time. On a stand that runs across several shows, a damaged tile is replaced without rebuilding the floor — which is exactly why the module beats the continuous surface.

The optional protective film helps in the worst phase, between laying the floor and opening: it comes off at the last moment, and the floor meets its first visitor exactly as it left production.

The fifteen finishes

The catalogue finishes cover three families: neutrals, timbers and concrete effects.

  • Neutral and metallic — snow white, warm white, deep black, metallic silver, light grey, mid grey / titanium, dark grey / anthracite.
  • Timbers — cream elm, bleached oak, touch elm, natural oak, grey oak, country oak.
  • Concrete — light concrete, dark concrete.

Further colours and finishes on request. If the stand has to carry a precise brand identity, the alternative route is the RAL-lacquered panel from our own paint shop.

Thicknesses, formats and quoting

Two thicknesses — 10 mm and 18 mm — and two formats, 1 × 1 m and 2 × 1 m. Thickness follows the substrate: 10 mm on a continuous, already load-bearing surface, 18 mm where the module spans wider supports or where footfall is heavy.

We quote floor tiles on request by finish, format and quantity, normally within 24 working hours and together with the rest of the order.

Standard sizes are always in stock. We normally ship within 7 days of order confirmation and in practice almost always sooner: in exhibition work the enquiry arrives from one day to the next, and we are set up for that.

  • Minimum order: none. We make single pieces.
  • Non-standard sizes: any size to drawing, with no cutting surcharge — you pay the price of the next standard size up.
  • Delivery: UK, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain, the Gulf and the whole of Europe, including direct to the exhibition halls within your build-up slot.
  • Prices: net, VAT excluded, 2026 price list, ex works Fontanelle (TV), Italy.

For deliveries outside Italy we agree the build-up slot, the customs paperwork where it applies and the transport documents with you in advance.

Frequently asked questions

How many finishes are there?

Fifteen in the catalogue, across neutrals, timbers and concrete effects, plus further colours and finishes on request.

Which thickness should I choose?

10 mm on a continuous, already load-bearing substrate, 18 mm where the supports are further apart or footfall is heavy.

Floors only?

No: raised floors, wall cladding and display surfaces on counters and plinths. They are modules, laid dry and replaceable one at a time.

What is the protective film?

An optional film that protects the surface between laying and opening: it is peeled off at the last moment.
