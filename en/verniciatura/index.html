In-house finishing

A department, not a subcontractor

Lacquering is the last operation and the one everyone sees. We run it in our own plant at Fontanelle, which is why we can answer for the whole chain: if a shade is off, it is our shade, not a supplier's.

We spray to any shade in the RAL range, gloss, satin or matt, plus UV gloss, brushing and polishing. The cycle is a full one — primer, intermediate sanding, top coat — on the individual piece.

Sheen

Gloss, satin or matt

The choice is made by looking at the light in the room, not at a sample under a workshop lamp. A north-facing room with little daylight carries a light matt far better than a gloss, which turns grey without light to reflect.

Choosing the sheen
SheenWhere it worksWhat to know
MattThe most requested today. Absorbs light, no reflections, hides irregularities in the wall or panel behind it.Shows fingerprints more around handles and grips.
SatinThe most balanced choice for everyday surfaces: a little reflection makes the surface easier to clean.No mirror effect, no matt fragility — the default we suggest when there is no constraint.
GlossAmplifies space and reflects the room.Unforgiving: it reveals every undulation in the substrate. It needs a surface prepared for that level.

Colour matching

Matching a shade you already have

If the reference has a RAL code, that is all we need. If it does not — a window frame painted years ago, an existing piece of furniture — send the physical sample: we identify the closest shade and tell you honestly how close it is. Between two adjacent RAL shades the difference shows on abutting surfaces and almost never on elements at opposite ends of a room. On a whole commission we confirm the match before the batch goes into production, not after.

Frequently asked questions

Which colours can you lacquer?

Any shade in the RAL range, gloss, satin or matt, plus UV gloss for very hard, brilliant surfaces. The cycle — primer, intermediate sanding, top coat — is run in-house on the individual piece.

Can you match a colour without a RAL code?

Yes: send the physical sample. We identify the closest shade and tell you honestly how close it is. On abutting surfaces the difference between two adjacent RAL shades shows; on elements at opposite ends of a room it almost never does.

Do you finish parts made by others?

Yes, once we have seen them. The substrate decides what a finish can achieve: on a poorly prepared or unstable surface even a perfect cycle will disappoint, and we prefer to say so before starting.

How long does a lacquering cycle take?

Six to eight weeks on doors in a RAL shade. The drying times inside the cycle cannot be compressed, which is why laminated products in standard sizes are always faster.

Matt or gloss?

Satin is the most balanced for everyday surfaces. Matt is currently the most requested and hides irregularities better. Gloss is striking but reveals every undulation in the substrate, so it needs a surface prepared for that level.

How is a lacquered surface cleaned?

A soft cloth and a neutral detergent. Solvents and abrasives dull a gloss finish and mark a matt one irreversibly.
