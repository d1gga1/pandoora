USA · Shipping and customs

Shipping exhibit panels from Italy to a US trade show

Production is the quick part. What decides whether the booth is on the show floor on time is freight, paperwork and the advance warehouse. Here is how it works, step by step, and who does what.

The Incoterm is written on the quote. If you already have a forwarder for your show freight, FCA or EXW keeps everything with them; if you do not, we can quote to a named place.

2. Ocean or air

Ocean freight is the default for a booth that will be reused or stored in the US: panels are light but bulky, and ocean freight is priced on volume far more gently than air. Count several weeks door to door, including the inland leg to the show city. Air freight is for the order that cannot wait — a late design change, a replacement piece — and it is priced on the greater of weight and volume.

For every ocean shipment to the US, the importer (or its broker) must file the Importer Security Filing (ISF, "10+2") with US Customs and Border Protection at least 24 hours before the cargo is loaded on the vessel. We send the data your broker needs — seller, manufacturer, commodity description, packing — as soon as the order is confirmed.

3. US customs: duty, carnet and wood rules

  • Customs broker. The US import is filed by a licensed customs broker on behalf of the importer of record (your exhibit house, your US entity or the show freight forwarder).
  • Duty. The rate depends on the HTS classification the broker assigns and on the tariff measures in force on the day of entry. US duties on European goods have changed several times since 2025, so we do not quote a duty rate: ask your broker to confirm it for the specific product before you ship.
  • ATA Carnet. The United States accepts ATA Carnets for commercial samples, professional equipment and exhibitions and fairs — for goods that are imported temporarily and re-exported. Consumables, giveaways and goods intended for sale cannot travel on a carnet. A booth that goes back to Europe after the show is a candidate; panels that stay in the US for reuse are imported normally.
  • Wood packaging (ISPM 15). Solid-wood pallets and crates must be heat-treated and stamped to ISPM 15; engineered boards such as OSB or plywood are exempt. Tell us the destination and we pack accordingly.
  • Lacey Act. Some wood products need a plant product declaration at entry. Your broker will tell you if the HTS code requires it; we provide the species and material data (spruce frame, MDF skins, paper honeycomb core).

Sources: U.S. Customs and Border Protection, ATA Carnet FAQs; CBP, Importer Security Filing. This is general information, not customs advice: your licensed broker has the final word.

4. At the show: advance warehouse, drayage and fire documents

Most shows offer an advance warehouse run by the general service contractor: freight is received in a set window before the show and delivered to the booth at move-in. Material handling (drayage) is billed by weight — per hundredweight (CWT, 100 lb) — with minimums, in and out. Light honeycomb walls make that line of the invoice smaller.

Put the fire documentation in the crate and in your email: US shows require flame-retardant booth materials and recommend keeping the certificate at the booth. Our fire-rated option is classified B-s1,d0 under EN 13501-1; see the Chicago page for an example of how a US venue words its rules.

Frequently asked questions

Does the US accept an ATA Carnet for a trade show booth?

Yes — the US accepts carnets for exhibitions and fairs, for goods that are re-exported after the show. Goods left in the US, sold or consumed need a normal import entry.

What is the ISF and who files it?

The Importer Security Filing ("10+2") is required by US Customs for ocean shipments and must be filed at least 24 hours before loading on the vessel, by the importer or its customs broker. We supply the data they need.

What import duty applies to honeycomb panels?

It depends on the HTS classification and on the tariff measures in force on the day of entry, which have changed repeatedly since 2025. Your customs broker confirms the rate for the specific product before shipping.

Do your crates meet ISPM 15?

Solid-wood packaging for the US must be ISPM 15 treated and stamped, while engineered boards such as OSB and plywood are exempt. Tell us the destination and we pack to the rule.

Can you deliver straight to the show?

Yes, through your forwarder or ours: to the advance warehouse, the show site within the move-in window, or your exhibit house.

Planning freight for a US show?

Tell us the show, the move-in date and your forwarder (if you have one). We quote production, packing and the Incoterm that suits you.
