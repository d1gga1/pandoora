Sales agents wanted worldwide

Pan.door.a is an Italian manufacturer of interior doors, honeycomb panels, exhibition stand components and furniture for homes and hotels, based near Treviso since 1987. Everything is made to measure from the architect's or designer's drawings. We are looking for experienced agents in every country, with an exclusive territory.

Who we are

We design, engineer, build and lacquer everything in our own factory in Fontanelle (TV), Italy, and ship worldwide. We want one agent or agency per country who can represent our whole range, or the product families that fit their existing clients.

What you would sell: four product families

Bespoke interior doors

33 models in laminate, RAL lacquer and CNC-engraved finishes: hinged, sliding, flush-to-wall, fire-rated and acoustic doors. Any size, opening and finish, also to the architect's own design.

See the doors →

Honeycomb panels

3,000 pieces in stock per standard size and custom sizes up to 6 m, fire-rated versions included. For exhibition stands, stage sets, furniture, retail and museum fit-outs.

See the panels →

Exhibition stand components

Stand walls, curved walls, raised floor platforms, stand doors and custom elements built to the stand designer's drawings, for sale or hire.

See the components →

Furniture for homes and hotels

Bedroom furniture, headboards, wardrobes, wall panelling, bathroom vanities and contract supply for hotels, serviced apartments and residential projects, built to the interior designer's project.

See the furniture →

Our strength: everything made to measure, from the project

Your clients usually work on a project by an architect, an interior designer or a stand designer. We do not make them choose from a catalogue: we start from their drawings and turn them into product. For an agent, that means saying yes to far more projects.

From design to shop drawings

Our technical office develops architects' and designers' projects in 3D and prepares the shop drawings for production.

In-house CNC production

Cutting, CNC machining, assembly and lacquering under one roof: non-standard sizes, shapes and details with no middlemen.

Any finish

Lacquer in any RAL colour, veneers and laminates. Doors, wall panelling and furniture matched in the same colour.

Sample before production

Your client sees and approves a sample or prototype before the full order goes into production.

From one piece to full contract

From a single custom piece to every door and every room of a hotel or residential project.

Worldwide shipping

Palletised and shipped across Europe, the UK, the Middle East, the USA and beyond.

What we offer

Exclusive territory

  • sales experience in the sector: doors and windows, furniture and contract, hospitality, building, exhibitions and events or wood components;
  • contacts with architects, interior designers, hotels or stand builders, the people who choose the supplier;

Frequently asked questions

What kind of contract do you offer?

An agency contract with an exclusive territory for the whole country and competitive commission. We consider both single-line and multi-line agents, provided the other mandates do not compete.

Which products would I sell?

Four product families: bespoke interior doors, honeycomb panels, exhibition stand components and furniture for homes and hotels. You can represent one family or all of them.

Why is made to measure an advantage for an agent?

Because you can say yes to projects by architects, interior designers, hotels and stand designers instead of offering a catalogue item. Non-standard sizes, shapes and finishes, shop drawings by our technical office and a sample before production: more projects closed and clients who come back.

Which countries are you looking for agents in?

All of them, one agent per country. There are local-language pages for Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Belgium, Luxembourg, Spain, Portugal, Croatia, Slovenia, Albania and Greece; for every other country, from the UK to the Middle East and the USA, apply with the form on this page.

Do I need experience?

Yes. We are looking for agents who have already sold in the sector: doors, furniture and contract, hospitality, building, exhibitions or wood components.

What support do you give?

Samples and catalogues, a trade price list, fast quotes, technical support from our office on your clients' projects and training at our factory in Fontanelle (TV), Italy.

Do you ship to my country?

Yes. We ship across Europe and worldwide; doors, panels and furniture travel on pallets to the client's site or warehouse.
