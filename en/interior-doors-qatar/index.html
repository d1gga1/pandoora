Shipping to Qatar

We ship by sea container to Hamad Port (around 3 weeks at sea) or by air for urgent items, with doors packed and labelled per floor or apartment. We prepare the export documents; import clearance is handled by your forwarder. Bigger projects travel better: combining doors, furniture components and panels in one container lowers the freight cost per piece.

Price and lead time

Complete door — leaf, frame, architraves and hardware — from 395 € ex works. Lacquered doors take 6–8 weeks to produce; transport is quoted separately.

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver to Doha?

Yes, by sea container to Hamad Port or by air. We prepare the export documents; customs clearance is handled by your forwarder.

Do you supply hotel projects?
