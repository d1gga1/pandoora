Coverings 2027–2029: the tile show moves to Orlando

Coverings announced that its 2027 edition runs April 6–9, 2027 at the Orange County Convention Center, the first of three consecutive years in Orlando. In 2026 the show was in Las Vegas, where the Gruppo Romani booth was built on our panels.

A tile booth asks a lot of its walls. Large-format porcelain slabs are heavy, and they are hung, not just leaned: for those walls we make the reinforced panel in 50 mm, with extra solid timber exactly where the fixings go. Tell us where the slabs hang on the elevation drawing and the reinforcement is placed there.

Florida humidity: choose the finish before you choose the freight

Raw MDF skins take up moisture from the air, and a crate that sits in a hot, humid warehouse in Florida is a humid place. For booths that will stay in the US or be stored between shows, we recommend a laminated or RAL lacquered finish over raw panels painted on site: the surface is sealed at the factory, the colour is consistent and the panel is easier to clean and reuse. For raw panels, keep the stretch wrap on until install.

Frequently asked questions

When and where is Coverings 2027?

April 6–9, 2027, at the Orange County Convention Center in Orlando, Florida. The organiser has announced three consecutive years in Orlando, 2027 to 2029.

Can your walls carry large porcelain slabs?

Yes, with the reinforced panel in 50 mm: extra solid timber is placed where the fixings go. Mark the hanging points on the elevation drawing and we build it in.

Which finish do you recommend for Florida?

A laminated or RAL lacquered finish, sealed at the factory, if the booth will be stored or reused in a humid climate. Raw panels are fine for single-use booths painted on site.

Can you deliver to the OCCC advance warehouse?

Yes — through your forwarder or ours, to the advance warehouse of the show contractor, your exhibit house or the show site within the move-in window.

Exhibiting at Coverings 2027 or another Orlando show?

Send the booth drawing and the move-in date: we reply the same day with a written quote in English — or in Italian, if your company is Italian.
