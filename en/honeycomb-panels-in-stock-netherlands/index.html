Stand panels in stock in the Netherlands

Alongside our factory in Italy we run a warehouse in Waalwijk, in the south of the Netherlands, holding thousands of raw honeycomb panels in standard sizes, ready to ship. For UK, Irish and Benelux contractors that means the panels are one ferry crossing away, not 1,450 km of road.

Why a warehouse in the Netherlands

One crossing from the UK

Waalwijk is about 95 km from Hook of Holland: overnight ferry to Harwich, and ExCeL London is roughly 115 km further. Northern venues go via the Rotterdam–Hull crossing.

Lower freight costs

Standard sizes no longer travel more than 1,000 km by road from Italy. A shorter run costs less and is far easier to time around your build-up slot.

Back-up during build-up

A panel damaged on unloading, a wall changed at the last minute: raw standard sizes sit close by and can be re-supplied quickly. Collection from the warehouse is also possible.

Frequently asked questions

Where is your warehouse in the Netherlands?

In Waalwijk, North Brabant, in the south of the Netherlands — about 95 km from the ferry port at Hook of Holland and 100 km from Amsterdam RAI.

Which panels are in stock in Waalwijk?

Thousands of raw honeycomb panels in the standard sizes of our price list, 40 mm thick: lengths from 2,500 to 5,000 mm and widths of 1,000, 750, 500 and 250 mm. Waalwijk holds the raw version only; finished panels ship from Italy.

Is shipping to the UK cheaper from Waalwijk?

For standard sizes, yes: instead of 1,400–1,950 km by road from Italy, the panels travel about 95 km to Hook of Holland and cross overnight to Harwich, which shows in the freight line of the quotation.

How quickly can you deliver from Waalwijk?

There is no production lead time: the panels are ready on the shelf. Delivery takes only the time to book the truck, load it and drive to your hall or store, plus the ferry crossing for the UK.

Can we collect the panels ourselves?

Yes, collection from the Waalwijk warehouse is possible. Tell us the sizes and quantities in advance and everything will be ready.

Do UK customs formalities still apply?

Yes. Great Britain is outside the EU customs union, so an export declaration and an import entry are needed wherever the panels ship from. We prepare our side of the documents with your consignee and broker.

Can you deliver to Ireland without going through the UK?

Yes: direct sailings from Rotterdam to Dublin keep the shipment inside the EU, with no customs step.

Panels needed for a UK or Benelux show?

Send the cutting list or the drawing with your build-up date: we tell you what can ship from Waalwijk and reply with a written quotation, usually within 24 working hours.
