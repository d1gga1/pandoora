Frequently asked questions

Questions before you order

How does ordering work?

Configure the product, add it to the cart and send the order on WhatsApp or by email. We get back to you to confirm the order, finish and delivery; with the 50% deposit the order is confirmed, then we survey the sizes and start production.

How do I pay?

50% deposit when the order is confirmed and 50% when the goods are ready: there are no online payments. The site survey takes place after confirmation and deposit. For details on the product or payment, message us on WhatsApp (green icon at the bottom right) or call +39 388 470 6887.

Does a custom size cost more?

Hardly ever: we are the manufacturer and build every door for your opening. Width to the centimetre costs nothing extra and the height is the same price up to a 2150 mm leaf. Only tall doors carry a surcharge: +35% from 2150 to 2500 mm, +60% over 2500 mm. Standard sizes are 60, 70, 80 and 90 × 210 cm.

What does the price include?

Leaf, wooden frame, plywood architraves, 3 concealed AGB hinges, AGB magnetic lock and standard handle: all included. Delivery and fitting are excluded: in Veneto and FVG you can add them for +€150 (laminate €395, lacquered €595). VAT excluded.

What warranty do the doors have?

2-year warranty on the door and 10 years on the concealed hinges and AGB magnetic lock. And before production we confirm sizes, finish and price with you.

How do I measure?

Measure the width and height of the opening on the finished wall and the wall thickness. The configurator works out the leaf, frame and clear passage. Measuring guide.

Can I choose any colour?

Yes, any colour in the RAL chart: the configurator shows the most popular or you can type the code.

How do I get the 5% discount?

Sign up to the newsletter: we email you the code straight away and it applies automatically in the cart, on every product, for 7 days from sign-up. It applies to every door, even with different sizes and finishes.
