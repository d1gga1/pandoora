Interior doors: information

Interior doors straight from the factory, with the price up front

Every door in the shop is made in our factory in Fontanelle (Treviso). Choose model, opening type, size and colour: the configurator instantly shows the price, the dimensioned drawing and what we make for your opening.

What is the difference between a laminate, lacquered and carved door?

Laminate from €245: high-resistance finish, the easiest to clean. RAL lacquered from €445: plain or with panels, in any colour of the RAL chart. Carved from €445: designs routed into the leaf and then lacquered. Prices VAT excluded, with frame, architraves and hardware.

Laminate, lacquered or carved: which to choose?

In short: laminate costs less and is the easiest to clean; lacquered and carved cost the same, come in any RAL colour and take 2 more weeks to produce.

Which opening types are available?

The models come as hinged doors (reversible left or right) and sliding doors: pocket sliding at the same price as hinged, surface-mounted or with the Magic system +€200. Flush-to-wall with invisible frame: primed to paint from €445, RAL lacquered from €645. Pivot-sliding +€190 on the laminate or lacquered price. Frequently asked questions

How much does an interior door cost?

From €245 + VAT for the laminate line, €445 for the lacquered line in any RAL colour and for the carved line, with wooden frame, plywood architraves, 3 concealed AGB hinges, AGB magnetic lock and handle. Delivery and fitting excluded; in Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia you can add them for +€150: laminate €395, lacquered €595. Full price list: interior door prices.

Do made-to-measure doors cost more?

Hardly ever: we are the manufacturer and build every door for your opening. Width to the centimetre costs nothing extra and the height is the same price up to a 2150 mm leaf. Only tall doors carry a surcharge: +35% from 2150 to 2500 mm, +60% over 2500 mm. Standard sizes are 60, 70, 80 and 90 × 210 cm.

How do I measure the opening?

Measure the width and height of the opening on the finished wall (plaster or plasterboard already done) and the wall thickness. We work out the rest: measuring guide.

How long does a lacquered door take?

Laminate doors take 3–4 weeks to make, lacquered doors 5–6 weeks.

Do you fit the doors?

Yes, with our own team: it is included in the price in Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia. How we work.

Can I have them without fitting?

Yes, the list price already excludes fitting: laminate from €245, lacquered from €445 + VAT. In Veneto and FVG delivery is included; outside Veneto and FVG shipping is quoted with the order, or you can collect them in Fontanelle (TV). Add fitting only if you need it, in the configurator under "Where is the door going?".

Do you ship interior doors throughout Italy and abroad?

Yes. In Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia survey, delivery and fitting by our team cost +€150 per door. Everywhere else in Italy and abroad we supply with shipping, without fitting: your fitter takes the sizes on our survey sheet, we make the doors to measure in Fontanelle (TV) and ship each door individually packed, with hardware and fitting instructions. The shipping cost is shown in the free quote, which we send within 24 working hours. How shipping works.
