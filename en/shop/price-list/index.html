How do I read the prices?

  • Standard sizes: the price in the table is the final price, VAT included.
  • Made to measure: price in proportion to width, height and depth compared with the standard size, plus 15%.
  • RAL lacquering: any RAL colour with a 20% surcharge on the unit.
  • Cube bookcases: the price shown is the starting structure; doors, drawers, shelves, baskets and base are added in the configurator.
  • Shipping: extra, quoted with the order. We confirm sizes and finish with you before production. Payment: 50% deposit when the order is confirmed, 50% when the goods are ready.

Price FAQ

How much do made-to-measure cabinets, bedside tables and TV units cost?

Cabinets start at €69 VAT included (modular open unit) and go up to €1789 for the TV wall unit. Each model has a fixed price for its standard size and, where stated, can be built to the centimetre.

How much does a made-to-measure wardrobe cost?

Pandoora wardrobes start at €309 VAT included (single-door column) and go up to €2,329 for the 250 cm fitted walk-in. Every model can be built to the centimetre with a 15% surcharge.

How much does a made-to-measure table top cost?

Table tops start at €389 VAT included: scratch-resistant HPL laminate or a round top lacquered in any RAL colour, cut and finished to size.

Do the prices include VAT and shipping?

Furniture prices include VAT; shipping is extra and quoted with the order, throughout Italy and Europe. Interior doors, on the other hand, are priced excluding VAT, without delivery and fitting: in Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia you can add them for +€150.

How is the price of made-to-measure furniture calculated?

We start from the price of the nearest standard size, scaled in proportion to width, height and depth, with a 15% surcharge. Lacquering in any RAL colour adds 20%.

Are there any discounts?

Yes: 5% with the newsletter code and 12% on matching furniture bundles. Discounts cannot be combined: the higher one always applies.

Where is the furniture made?

In the Pan.door.a factory in Fontanelle (Treviso), where the company has worked with wood since 1987. Pandoora manufactures directly; it does not resell.
