Porcelain washbasins: information

Made-to-measure washbasins in the same slab as your floor

We make the washbasins from porcelain stoneware slabs, joined with 45° mitred edges: length from 60 to 250 cm and beyond, custom depth, single or double basin, square or with a sloped bottom (ramp).

How much does it cost?

Medium sizes from 60 to 80 cm: €800. Large sizes from 100 to 120 cm: €1,100. Extra-large and custom sizes (150, 180, 250 cm) and double washbasins: custom price on request. Prices VAT excluded.

Which finishes are available?

Since they are made to measure, we make them in any finish and colour, including travertine, marble and natural stone effects. Choose from the porcelain tile catalogue or send us a photo. Learn more: made-to-measure porcelain washbasins and tops.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a made-to-measure porcelain washbasin cost?

From 60 to 80 cm €800, from 100 to 120 cm €1,100, VAT excluded. Extra-large sizes and double washbasins quoted on request.

Can you make a double washbasin?

Yes, single or double, with a square or sloped basin: double washbasins and sizes over 120 cm have a custom price on request.

Can I choose the finish?

Yes, any finish and colour, including travertine, marble and natural stone effects.

Do you install it?

Yes, in Milan and its province, in Veneto and in Friuli Venezia Giulia. Elsewhere we ship it packed.
