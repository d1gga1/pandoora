Frequently asked questions

Questions before you order

How does ordering work?

Choose the grade and the square metres, add to cart and send the request on WhatsApp or by email. We confirm the square metres available, the exact price and delivery before going ahead.

How do I pay?

50% deposit when the order is confirmed and 50% when the goods are ready: there are no online payments. The site survey takes place after confirmation and deposit. For details on the product or payment, message us on WhatsApp (green icon at the bottom right) or call +39 388 470 6887.

How much does it cost per square metre?

First grade and 1°C from €18 to €21/m², second grade and S grade from €12 to €14/m², VAT excluded and shipping extra. The exact price depends on the lot.

What does second grade mean?

These are slabs with small aesthetic differences compared with first grade (shade, minor surface flaws), at a lower price: suitable for cellars, garages, utility rooms or anyone looking to save.

How many square metres are there?

These are stock lots in limited quantities: send us the name of the shade and we'll tell you straight away how many square metres there are of each grade.

Do you also lay the tiles?

Yes, in Milan and its province, Monza, Pavia, in Veneto and in Friuli Venezia Giulia. Elsewhere we ship the material.
