Furniture: information

Furniture for the whole home, also made to measure

Bedside tables, TV units, sideboards, cube bookcases, hallway and bathroom furniture made in our factory in Fontanelle (Treviso). Choose a standard size or enter your own: the price updates instantly.

How do the cube bookcases work?

Alveare bookcases in honeycomb panel are built compartment by compartment: columns, rows, compartment size, depth, doors, drawers, shelves and base.

How much do they cost?

Prices VAT included, shipping extra (bookcases include a wall fixing kit). Made to measure: 15% surcharge; RAL lacquering: 20%. Full price list, size by size →

Frequently asked questions

Can the furniture be made to measure?

Yes, the models marked "Made to measure" are built to the centimetre within the limits shown on the product page.

What are the cube bookcases made of?

Honeycomb-core panel: light and rigid, the same we produce for exhibition stands.

Do the prices include VAT?

Yes, for all furniture.
