How does ordering work?

Configure the product, add it to the cart and send the order on WhatsApp or by email. We get back to you to confirm the order, finish and delivery; with the 50% deposit the order is confirmed, then we survey the sizes and start production.

How do I pay?

50% deposit when the order is confirmed and 50% when the goods are ready: there are no online payments. The site survey takes place after confirmation and deposit. For details on the product or payment, message us on WhatsApp (green icon at the bottom right) or call +39 388 470 6887.

Does the price include VAT?

Yes, furniture prices include VAT; shipping is extra and we quote it with the order.

Can it be made to measure?

Yes: enter your sizes in the configurator and the price updates instantly.
