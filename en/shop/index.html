Shop FAQ

Are the shop's products made in Italy?

Yes. We make doors and furniture ourselves in our factory in Fontanelle (Treviso), as we have since 1987, lacquering included, with no outsourced steps. The porcelain tiles and stoneware washbasins are made in Italy.

What does the Pandoora shop sell?

Interior doors, wardrobes, furniture, cube bookcases, table tops, porcelain tiles and made-to-measure porcelain washbasins. We make the doors and furniture ourselves in Fontanelle (Treviso), Italy, since 1987; the porcelain tiles are stock lots.

How much do the interior doors cost?

From €245 + VAT for a laminate door and from €445 + VAT for a RAL lacquered or carved door, with frame, architraves and hardware. In Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia delivery and fitting cost €150 extra per door.

Can I order made to measure?

Yes. Doors are built to the centimetre at no extra cost up to a 2150 mm leaf; furniture marked as made to measure costs 15% more than the price in proportion to the size. Only tall doors carry a surcharge: +35% from 2150 to 2500 mm, +60% over 2500 mm.

How do I order and pay?

Configure the product, send the order on WhatsApp or by email and we contact you to confirm it. You pay a 50% deposit on confirmation and 50% when the goods are ready: there are no online payments.

Do you ship abroad?

Yes, we ship throughout Italy and Europe, with shipping quoted in the estimate. We do the fitting in Veneto and Friuli Venezia Giulia and, for tiles and washbasins, in Milan and its province.
