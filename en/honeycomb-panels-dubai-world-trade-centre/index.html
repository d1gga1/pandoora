Why honeycomb panels are new to many Dubai stand builders

In much of the Gulf a custom stand is still built as one-off carpentry: timber studs clad in plywood or MDF, filled, painted and very often skipped when the show closes. It works, but it costs workshop days before every show, heavy trucks into the hall and a great deal of waste.

A honeycomb panel (also called a hollow-core panel) is a finished wall element: a frame in knot-free finger-jointed spruce, a perforated honeycomb core and two MDF skins bonded under press. It weighs a fraction of a solid wall of the same size, stays flat, joins to the next panel with alignment strips and I·L·T·X connectors, and after the show it is stacked and stored for the next build. In Europe it has been the standard for custom stands for decades; in Dubai it is still an advantage for the builders who adopt it first.

What we supply for a stand

  • standard panel PSL for the walls, 40 or 50 mm, up to 6,000 mm high;
  • the fire-rated finish wherever the venue regulations ask for it, with the documentation;
  • stand doors, assembled and with the hardware fitted, reversible, with porthole or panic bar;
  • levelling platforms, pre-drilled for the adjustable feet, for the raised floor;
  • folding columns, which travel flat — a direct saving when freight is charged on volume;
  • alignment strips, I·L·T·X connectors and cover strips for dry assembly.

Planning backwards from the build-up slot

Dubai changes the planning, not the product. We manufacture in Fontanelle, in the province of Treviso; standard sizes are in stock and normally ship within 7 days of order confirmation. From there the variable is freight: sea freight to Jebel Ali is counted in weeks, air freight in budget. For a first stock of panels that will stay in Dubai and be reused show after show, sea freight is the obvious choice; air freight is for the order that cannot wait.

Dubai is outside the EU customs union: we issue the export declaration and commercial documents, and the import is cleared by your consignee or clearing agent — the UAE applies a standard 5% duty on the CIF value plus 5% VAT. Tell us the consignee and the agent at order stage and we prepare the paperwork around them. Delivery is to your workshop or warehouse in Dubai, or to the venue through your forwarder.

Fire documentation needs the same foresight: decide the fire-rated finish before the container is loaded, not when the venue asks for the certificate. See certificates and documentation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a honeycomb panel for exhibition stands?

A finished wall element made of a solid spruce frame, a honeycomb core and two MDF skins. It is light, flat and reusable, and joins to other panels with connectors for dry assembly.

Do you deliver to Dubai World Trade Centre?

Yes, by sea or air freight to Dubai, delivered to your workshop or warehouse or to the venue through your forwarder, in time for your build-up slot.

How long does delivery take?

Standard sizes are in stock and normally ship within 7 days of order confirmation. Sea transit to Jebel Ali and customs clearance come on top, so plan backwards from the build-up date.

Are the panels available fire-rated?

Yes, with the fire-performance documentation supplied at no extra cost. Doors, platforms and lacquered panels are available fire-rated too.

Can the panels stay in Dubai and be reused?

Yes — that is the point of the system. Panels are stacked and stored after the show and reused for the next build, so the freight is paid once.

Do you work to the stand builder’s drawing?

Yes, we make to measure, including single pieces, with no surcharge for non-standard sizes: you pay the price of the next standard size up.

Pricing a stand in Dubai?

Send us the plan, the sizes and the quantities: we reply with a written quote, normally within 24 working hours, in English, French, German or Italian.
