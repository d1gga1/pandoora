Coverings 2026: an Italian booth built on our panels

Gruppo Romani, one of Italy's ceramic tile groups, exhibited its brands at Coverings 2026, held at the Las Vegas Convention Center from March 30 to April 2, 2026. The booth — two volumes, a terracotta front with tall arched openings and a black box lit by a neon line — was built on our honeycomb panels: straight walls, curved elements for the arches and door frames, numbered in Italy and crated for the trip.

That is the kind of project honeycomb panels are made for: a large, finished-looking structure that has to be shipped across an ocean, put up in a few days by a crew that has never seen it, and taken down again without damage.

The Las Vegas venues

  • Las Vegas Convention Center (LVCC) — home to CES, NAB Show, the SEMA Show and World of Concrete, and to Coverings in 2026. Very large halls, very large island booths, and heights to match.
  • Venetian Expo — the expo center attached to The Venetian: jewelry, design, tech and many association shows.
  • Mandalay Bay Convention Center — at the south end of the Strip, with a busy calendar of mid-size and large shows.

Show calendars change from year to year: always check the official show website and the exhibitor manual for dates, height limits and fire rules.

What we supply for a Las Vegas booth

Hard walls

Standard panels in 40 or 50 mm, cut to 8 ft for inline booths or up to 19 ft 8 in for island structures. Raw for paint or graphics, laminated white or black, or RAL lacquered.

Curves and arches

Curved panels with radii from 250 to 1,000 mm (about 10 to 39 in), as on the Coverings 2026 booth. Built on your drawing.

Booth doors

Pre-hung in their frames, reversible, sliding or double; with porthole or panic bar on request. Stand doors.

Platforms

Levelling platforms pre-drilled for adjustable feet, to raise the floor over cables. Levelling platforms.

Low walls and counters

Freestanding low wall modules and structural elements for reception counters and product plinths.

Connectors

Alignment strips and I·L·T·X steel connectors for dry assembly — no glue, no screws through the face.

Planning a Las Vegas show from Italy

Work backwards from the target move-in date in the exhibitor manual. Production is quick — 2–3 working days from stock, 7 days for custom sizes, 10–12 days for RAL lacquer — so the variable is freight: ocean freight to a US port and inland trucking to Nevada take weeks; air freight takes days and costs more. Most exhibit houses ship to the advance warehouse of the general service contractor, which accepts freight in a set window before the show; shipping direct to the show site is the alternative when the schedule is tight.

Las Vegas halls are dry, air-conditioned spaces, and the panels travel in crates, so the finish arrives as it left. If the booth will be reused in the US, ask us about storage-friendly crating from the start: the same crates can go back to the exhibit house after the show. Customs, ISF and the ATA Carnet are covered in shipping panels to the USA.

Frequently asked questions

Have you already supplied a booth in Las Vegas?

Yes. The Gruppo Romani booth at Coverings 2026, at the Las Vegas Convention Center (March 30 – April 2, 2026), was built with our honeycomb walls and curved elements, made and crated in Italy.

Can you make the arches and curved walls?

Yes — curved panels with radii from 250 to 1,000 mm (about 10 to 39 inches), to your drawing. It is one of the things few panel makers do for exhibits.

What height can the walls be?

Up to 6,000 mm (19 ft 8 in) in one piece. Inline booths are usually capped at 8 ft by the show rules; island booths allow more, but the exact limit is in each exhibitor manual.

Where do you deliver in Las Vegas?

To your exhibit house, to the advance warehouse of the show contractor, or to the show site within the target move-in window — through your forwarder or ours.

Do you work directly with US exhibit houses?

Yes. We supply the panels, connectors and doors; the exhibit house or I&D contractor builds the booth. Quotes and technical notes are in English.
