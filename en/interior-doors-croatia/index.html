Why buy interior doors from Italy when you build in Croatia

Our workshop is in Fontanelle, thirty kilometres from the Italian–Slovenian corridor that leads straight into Croatia. Rijeka is 380 km away, Zagreb 500, Split 750. That is closer than most Croatian buyers assume, and it is the reason the Croatian market works for us the way the Italian one does: one day of transport, no customs, no currency risk.

What you are buying is not a catalogue product with a longer delivery address. Every leaf is built to the clear opening it will live in: a finger-jointed solid timber perimeter frame, a honeycomb core and two facings bonded under press. It is the same construction we have used for forty years on exhibition panels up to six metres long — which is why a 280 cm leaf is normal work here rather than a special order with a surcharge.

What we build

Hinged doors

Three concealed hinges adjustable on three axes, magnetic latch, perimeter seal, reversible hand. Standard widths 60–90 cm, any non-standard size to drawing.

Flush-to-wall doors

Leaf flush with the wall on a concealed aluminium frame, no architraves. Lacquered in the wall colour or supplied ready to paint with it.

Pocket sliding doors

The leaf disappears into the wall. The metal sub-frame ships ahead of the doors so it goes in while the wall is still open.

Surface sliding doors

A track above the opening and a leaf that slides along the wall: no building work, ideal for fitting out a finished apartment.

Acoustic doors

Solid core instead of honeycomb, seals on the rebates and a drop seal under the leaf — for bedrooms, home offices and hotel rooms.

Fire-rated doors

EI certified doors for corridors, shafts and hotel guestrooms, supplied with their fire documentation.

Finishes: laminate, RAL lacquer in matt, satin or gloss, routed Superior patterns, and glass. The full range is in the doors catalogue.

Logistics: what a Croatian delivery actually involves

Since 2013 Croatia has been an EU member and since 2023 it uses the euro. For a supplier in Italy that removes almost every complication:

  • No customs. Goods move inside the single market — no import declaration, no EUR.1, no broker.
  • No currency risk. The quotation is in euro and the euro is what you pay.
  • Reverse charge for businesses. A valid EU VAT number means an invoice without Italian VAT.

From enquiry to installation

  1. Door schedule and quotation. Numbered openings, approximate sizes, opening direction, finish. Written quotation within 24 working hours, itemised: leaf, frame, architraves, hardware, finish, transport.
  2. Measuring sheet. Filled in opening by opening by your joiner: clear opening, wall thickness, hand and finished floor level — the most frequent and most expensive mistake.
  3. Sample and production. Finish sample where needed, then 6–8 weeks for RAL lacquer, less for laminate.
  4. Packing and delivery. Individually packed, labelled by room, delivered to site.
  5. Installation. By your joiner or contractor, with our technical support.

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver interior doors to Croatia?

Yes, to the whole country. We manufacture in Fontanelle (Treviso), north-east Italy. Croatia is in the EU and the eurozone, so there is no customs clearance, no import declaration and no currency conversion. A truck reaches Rijeka or Zagreb in one day, Split in two, Dubrovnik in two to three.

How much do the doors cost?

From €195 per door excluding VAT: a laminate leaf in a standard size, with frame, architraves and basic hardware — a door ready to install, not just the leaf. RAL-lacquered and routed models cost more. We quote opening by opening.

Can you make doors taller than 210 cm?

Yes, and it is routine work for us. Leaves up to 280 cm are made on the normal production line; above that we solve it opening by opening, sometimes with a fixed transom. The finger-jointed perimeter frame is what keeps a tall leaf flat.

Who installs the doors in Croatia?

Your joiner or main contractor. We send a measuring sheet to be filled in opening by opening, the hardware, fitting instructions and technical support by phone or video call. Doors arrive labelled room by room, ready to install.

How is VAT handled?

Companies with a valid EU VAT number are invoiced without Italian VAT under the reverse charge. Private buyers are invoiced according to the distance-selling rules, with the VAT shown in the quotation.

What is the lead time?

Six to eight weeks of production for RAL-lacquered doors, less for laminate in standard sizes, plus one to three days of transport. The quotation states the timeline in dates, not only in weeks.

Do you work with hotels and rental properties?

Yes. For apartments and hotels laminate is usually the right choice for durability, with the same shade and the same hardware on every door. Fire-rated EI doors are supplied with their certification documents.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We make a single door as well as an order of eighty, although on very small orders transport weighs more per unit.

Doors for a project in Croatia?

Send the door schedule or the drawings: we reply with a written quotation, normally within 24 working hours, in English. We also work in Croatian.
